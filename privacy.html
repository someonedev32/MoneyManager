Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-13

FinanceWiser is designed to work offline and keep your financial information on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and your choices.

1) Information We Collect

We do not collect your personal financial data (transactions, balances, categories, accounts) to our servers.

  • Data you enter: Transactions, accounts, categories, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Optional imports: If you import data (e.g., CSV), the file is processed on your device.
  • Support contact (optional): If you us, we will receive the information you include in your message (such as your address and any attachments).

2) How We Use Information

  • To provide core app functionality (tracking balances, transactions, and reports).
  • To respond to support requests if you contact us.

Your app data is stored on your device. You are responsible for protecting access to your device (e.g., using a passcode/biometrics). No method of storage is 100% secure, but we aim to follow reasonable security practices for the app.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with third parties. If you choose to export your data, you control where it is saved and shared.

Note: If you integrate optional external services in future versions, this policy will be updated accordingly.

Please also review our Terms & Conditions .

The app is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
