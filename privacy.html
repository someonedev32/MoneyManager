Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

FinanceWise is an offline personal finance tracker designed to keep your financial information on your device. You can manage expenses, income, accounts, categories, and reports without creating an account or using cloud storage. The app also provides data import and export functionality and optional Pro features.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FinanceWise handles, how it is used, and your choices.

1) Information We Collect

FinanceWise does not collect or upload your personal financial records to our own servers. Your financial information is stored locally on your device.

  • Financial data: Transactions, income, expenses, balances, accounts, categories, currencies, and related settings are stored locally on your device.
  • App preferences: Settings, preferences, and app configuration are stored locally.
  • Import and export files: Files you choose to import or export are processed through the app's local data functionality. You control which files are selected and where exported files are saved.
  • Support contact: If you contact us, we receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address, message, and any attachments.
  • Subscription information: If you purchase or restore FinanceWise Pro, Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase-related information needed to manage and verify your subscription entitlement. This does not include your financial transactions entered into the app.

2) How We Use Information

  • To provide core app functionality, including tracking income, expenses, accounts, balances, and financial reports.
  • To import, export, and restore your financial data when you choose to use those features.
  • To manage and verify access to FinanceWise Pro features.
  • To respond to support requests that you submit.
  • To maintain app functionality and address technical issues.

3) Data Storage and Security

Your financial records are stored locally on your device. FinanceWise does not require an account, does not use cloud storage for your financial records, and does not automatically upload your transactions to our servers.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and your exported backup files. You can use your device's passcode and supported biometric authentication to help protect access.

Exported files may contain sensitive financial information. Anyone who obtains access to an exported file may be able to read its contents. Store and share backup files carefully.

We use reasonable measures to protect information handled through services used for app functionality and subscription management. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

4) Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored financial records with third parties.

FinanceWise does not automatically transmit your financial records to us or to RevenueCat. Subscription-related information may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as necessary to manage purchases and subscription entitlements.

If you export a file, use the iOS share sheet, or send information to another app or person, you control that action and the destination of the information.

5) Third-Party Services

FinanceWise uses third-party services for App Store purchases and subscription management. These services may process limited information necessary to provide their functionality.

  • Apple App Store: Handles in-app purchases, payment processing, subscription management, and purchase restoration through your Apple account.
  • RevenueCat: Supports subscription entitlement management and purchase verification. RevenueCat may process purchase-related information, app user identifiers, and subscription status necessary to provide its service.

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their respective privacy policies and terms.

FinanceWise financial records, including your transactions, account balances, and budgets, are not intentionally sent to RevenueCat for subscription management.

Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

6) Purchases and Subscriptions

FinanceWise may offer paid Pro features through in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. RevenueCat is used to help manage and verify subscription entitlements. We do not receive your full payment card details through FinanceWise.

Subscription status may be checked to determine whether Pro features are available. Apple and RevenueCat may process purchase records, subscription status, and identifiers required for this functionality.

You can manage, cancel, or review eligible subscriptions through your Apple account settings. Deleting FinanceWise does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

Please also review our Terms & Conditions .

7) Children's Privacy

FinanceWise is not intended for children under 13, or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8) Your Choices

  • Access and correction: You can view and manage your financial records directly within FinanceWise.
  • Delete data: You can delete individual records within the app or remove locally stored app data by uninstalling the app. Uninstalling may permanently remove data that has not been exported or backed up.
  • Export: You can export your financial data through the app's Settings. You control where the exported file is saved and who receives it.
  • Import: You choose which files to import. Imported data is processed locally by the app.
  • Subscriptions: You can manage or cancel your subscription through your Apple account settings.
  • Support information: If you contact us, you may request assistance regarding information included in your support communication.

9) Offline Functionality

FinanceWise core financial tracking, reports, and local data import/export functionality are designed to work offline. Your financial records do not require an internet connection to be stored or accessed locally.

An internet connection may be required for App Store purchases, subscription verification or restoration, and other Apple services. Subscription-related functionality may not be available until the required services can be reached.

10) Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, its features, or applicable requirements. We will revise the “Last updated” date above when changes are made.

Please review this policy periodically to stay informed about how FinanceWise handles information.
